## 🛡️ Architect's Defense (Capstone Tips)

> **Why do we include Marketing Webs in the Repo?**
> "A SaaS product like SIGA is not just the Dashboard. By including the **Commercial Web** and the **Landing** in the Monorepo, we ensure that any change in branding or pricing plans is reflected atomically across the ecosystem, maintaining visual and business coherence."
>
> **Is the WebApp a microservice?**
> "Technically, it is a frontend artifact, but in our architecture we treat it with the same rigor as a microservice: it has its own Dockerfile, its own deployment pipeline, and it consumes versioned contracts from the backend."
